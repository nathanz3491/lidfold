Welcome to lidfold

Your desktop folds away as you close the lid, the way Mac Duo does it on a MacBook, using your webcam instead of a hinge sensor.

1
Calibrate once Step out of the camera's view, tilt the lid smoothly through three stops, then click the edge of the laptop base. About a minute.
2
Pick your look Click the lidfold icon in the taskbar tray for effects, blur and where the effect starts.
3
Close the lid Pause any time with Ctrl+Alt+Shift+F.
