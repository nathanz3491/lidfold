# Third-party notices

## Mac Duo

lidfold ports parts of [Mac Duo](https://github.com/DhananjayBhosale/MacDuo):

- the Duo and Ghost shaders (`duo()` and `ghost()` in `lidfold/shaders.py`);
- the fold curves (`Visual.at` in `lidfold/fold.py`);
- the tracking approach (`LidTracker` in `lidfold/fold.py`): rebasing on the
  resting angle, and clearing when the lid is held still.
